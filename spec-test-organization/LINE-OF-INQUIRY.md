# Line of inquiry — where the questioning stood when work stopped

Recorded 2026-08-28. This is the thread of requirements elicitation that was
open when Jonathan redirected the effort. If the ideas in `PLAN.md` are ever
resumed, resume from here rather than from the plan itself: the plan was
written *before* these questions were answered, which is why it was set aside.

## How this thread started

The AI partner wrote `PLAN.md` after four multiple-choice questions and a
document analysis pass. Jonathan pushed back: *"I thought we were going to
discuss this first, as we need to figure out what my requirements are."* The
plan was demoted to a strawman and a requirements conversation began — in
small rounds, with anything that sounded like a requirement to be captured as
a candidate for Jonathan to mark up (keep / sharpen / drop) before anything
was designed against it.

## The three questions that were on the table (never answered)

1. **Where does it hurt today?** The last real incident where taking an idea
   from head toward something implementable broke down — what actually went
   wrong? (Asked because the real incident matters more than the general
   theory; every prior attempt designed against the theory.)

2. **What decision would the visual surface serve?** A dashboard earns its
   existence by the decisions it lets you make at a glance. When Jonathan
   imagines looking at it, what is he deciding in that moment — what to work
   on next, whether a spec is trustworthy enough to hand to an agent, whether
   the project is drifting from his intent? (Asked because "visualize maturity
   and completeness" names a rendering, not a purpose.)

3. **What must it feel like when it's working?** Picture a session growing a
   spec that went *well* — what was Jonathan doing, what was the AI doing,
   what was absent that's usually present? (Asked because agent-method's
   purpose statement says "enjoyable and flow-preserving," and no artifact
   list can be checked against a feeling that was never described.)

Plus one process ruling that was pending: whether this requirements work
should live inside agent-method's candidate/markup/promotion process or as a
separate track. Overtaken by events — Jonathan chose to restart agent-method
clean instead.

## What answered the thread indirectly

Jonathan's redirect itself was a partial answer, especially to question 1:
the abstraction and process ceremony *was* the hurt — "it became
overwhelming" and led to a long break. That observation and its consequences
are captured in `PERCEIVED-INTENT.md`.

## If resuming

Questions 2 and 3 remain genuinely open. Question 1 deserves re-asking with
the 2026-08-28 answer as a starting point, not a final one.
