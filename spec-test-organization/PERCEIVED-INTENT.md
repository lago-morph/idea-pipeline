> **Unratified.** This is a mirror, not a source of truth — its only authority

## The moment this was written from

Jonathan returned after a long break, taken because the work "became
overwhelming." He described the existing material as "very abstract" and
redirected from process design toward a concrete experiment. Everything below
is read from that redirect and the feedback around it.

## What he seems to want

**Concrete before abstract.** Typed artifacts holding real content, defined
by making them — not schemas, requirements registers, or process documents
designed in the abstract. The starting artifact set he named: a project
vision document, use cases, component definitions, and typed interfaces
specified in a language-neutral way.

**A graph, not a line.** Artifacts organized as linked data — one node per
artifact — rather than one linear markdown file. Links are two-way
(traversable in both directions) and typed, optionally directional. Initial
link types he named: *is part of* (directional), *depends on* (directional),
*is related to* (non-directional).

**Experimentation without ceremony.** "Without a lot of process and
ceremony," in his words. Process should emerge from felt friction during real
use, not be installed up front. The known risk — named and accepted — is that
defining the type system could itself become the new over-engineering; the
antidote he chose is to start with a small artifact set and "see where that
takes us."

**Previous work as ore, not obligation.** The first methodology attempt and
the requirements-first rebuild are both archived — kept to mine for source
material and schema later, but out of default context so they impose no
weight. Two archived attempts is a pattern worth watching: the underlying
need may be a *smaller unit of commitment*, which a graph serves naturally —
it grows one node at a time and nothing demands completeness.

**Discussion before deliverables.** A polished plan delivered before his
requirements were understood broke the flow and prompted the pushback that
ended `PLAN.md`. The wanted rhythm: talk in small rounds, reflect
understanding back, invite critique, and only then produce.

**Intent authorship stays with him.** The AI drafts, captures, critiques, and
reflects; what Jonathan actually wants is his alone to state. AI renderings
of his intent (this document included) are labeled as inference until he
marks them up.

**A visual way in, eventually.** He takes in information visually; a visual
surface over the artifact graph is latent in everything above — but it was
part of the plan that was set aside, so it waits until the experiment creates
a real need for it.

## What he asked for next

One jointly crafted prompt to kickstart the artifacts-first experiment in the
cleaned agent-method repository, starting from the artifact set and link
types above.
