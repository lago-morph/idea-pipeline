# agent instruction

**Re-read for attribution drift when writing on the author's behalf.** After drafting anything that speaks for the author — a transcription, a session capture, a PR description of their idea — do one full pass hunting for: your inference presented as their words; a hedge they used ("probably", "for now", "with a little more nuance") that you dropped; "decided" where they stated a position; an idea that quietly vanished. Fix each before showing it.

*Grounded in: the Appendix A re-read caught three; the author caught a fourth.*

# justification

The re-read of Appendix A before its first commit caught three places where the agent had put words in the author's mouth: their wife's comment recorded as "adopted as the brief" when they had said "with a little more nuance"; an inference ("git-tracked-or-not subsumes the flag") stated as theirs; and a table mixing agreed and open items without saying so. The author then caught a fourth class — decisions versus positions — that the pass had not been looking for. The cost of not re-reading is that the author has to catch it, and until they do the document misrepresents them. The cost is one read with a short checklist.
