# agent instruction

**Tell a subagent it may correct the brief.** "When a brief asserts something about a source — its fidelity, its scope, what it contains — instruct the reader to verify that assertion against the source and to correct you if it is wrong. Read its correction before using its findings."

*Grounded in: a brief that told a reader to treat part of its file as low-fidelity; the reader found a note superseding that caveat and said so.*

# justification

A brief in this session instructed a reader to mark everything before a certain point in its file as summary-fidelity rather than verbatim, because an earlier version of the file had carried that caveat. The reader read the file, found a section stating the document had since been rebuilt from a full log by a deterministic script with no compaction, and opened its report by correcting the brief. That correction materially upgraded the source: the sections carrying the owner's own statements were verbatim, not summary. Had the reader followed the brief obediently, every finding from those sections would have been discounted for no reason. One clause in the brief buys a reader who checks the orchestrator's assumptions instead of inheriting them.
