# agent instruction

**Own project and own experience are one source.** "When material from a practitioner's own project and that practitioner's own stated experience both support a claim, they corroborate each other not at all. Count them as one source for any threshold, and say so where the claim is recorded."

*Grounded in: two anti-patterns held at candidate because their two evidence lines were the owner's project and the owner's own statement.*

# justification

This session ingested the corpus owner's own project as a source, and separately recorded his stated position from conversation as own experience. Counted as documents, two new anti-patterns each had two evidence lines and cleared the promotion bar. Counted as perspectives, both lines were one person, and promoting on them would have put one practitioner's word into the injectable checklist wearing the costume of corroboration. Holding them at candidate cost nothing — the pages exist, they are findable, and the status accurately says what is known. The asymmetry is stark: the check is a single question asked once per promotion, and the failure mode is a corpus that silently launders self-agreement into consensus.
