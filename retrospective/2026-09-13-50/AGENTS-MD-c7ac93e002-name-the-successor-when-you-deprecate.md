# agent instruction

**Name the successor when you deprecate.** "When retiring a rule, pattern, or check, state in the deprecation note where each surviving part of its function went. If no successor exists, say that explicitly rather than leaving the gap unremarked."

*Grounded in: two deprecations whose surviving functions were distributed across four other pages.*

# justification

Two pages were deprecated in this session on the owner's judgement. A reader of the supporting source pointed out that the defects which had actually surfaced were not the kind the deprecated practice would have caught — and warned that deprecating without naming a replacement would remove a function from the corpus while leaving nothing in its place. The deprecation notes were written to name, part by part, which pages absorbed which function. Writing them forced the question "what catches this now?" at the only moment the answer was still fresh. The marginal cost is two sentences per deprecation; the cost of omitting them is a corpus that quietly loses capabilities and cannot say when.
