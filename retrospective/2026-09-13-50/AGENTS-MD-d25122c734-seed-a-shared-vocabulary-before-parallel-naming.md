# agent instruction

**Seed a shared vocabulary before parallel naming.** "Before dispatching parallel agents that may propose names, give every brief the existing vocabulary and require reuse, with new names flagged explicitly. Reconcile collisions before anything is written."

*Grounded in: three readers proposing the same new idea as `hand-fixed-validation`, `fix-source-not-environment` and `verify-from-clean-state`.*

# justification

Three readers working on three slices of one source independently identified the same missing concept and proposed three different names for it. The briefs had carried the existing vocabulary and required reuse, which is why the collision was visible and cheap: the three proposals arrived side by side and merged into one page before anything was committed. Without the shared vocabulary, the same three findings would have mapped onto three different existing pages as well, scattering the evidence. This rule was proposed in an earlier retrospective and never assembled into force; the collision recurred in this session, which is the second data point and the argument for adopting it rather than proposing it again.
