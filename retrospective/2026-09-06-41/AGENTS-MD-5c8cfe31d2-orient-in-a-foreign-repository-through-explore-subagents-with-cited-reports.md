# agent instruction

**Orient in a foreign repository through Explore subagents with cited reports.** To understand another repository's objectives and status, clone it to `/tmp`, split it into two or three bounded reading assignments, and have Explore subagents return reports under 1500 words with a file path for every claim, a vocabulary list, and no critique. Read yourself only the few files whose exact shape you must mirror.

*Grounded in: the two agent-method reports that fed the spine without loading 500 KB of notes into the main context.*

# justification

Agent-method's non-archive content is about 500 KB across 109 files. Two Explore subagents read it in full and returned reports of about 1500 words each, with file paths on every claim and a twenty-term vocabulary list. Those two reports, plus four small files read directly because their exact conventions had to be mirrored, were enough to design a structure that slots into agent-method's own patterns. The main context spent perhaps 8 KB on the repository instead of 500 KB. The brief's constraints did the work: a fixed report outline, a word cap, "do not critique", "quote with file paths", and a vocabulary section. The vocabulary section in particular is what let every later document use agent-method's words rather than coined ones.
