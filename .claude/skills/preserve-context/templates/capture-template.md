**What this is.** A capture of the ideas exchanged in [the session /
the transcript at PATH], grouped by topic rather than by time. It holds
what was said — including suggestions that were modified or dropped — so
the whole context can be picked up again later. Both voices are
compressed; key phrases are quoted.

**What this is not.** Not a design, not a prioritized list, not a set of
decisions. [The only decisions made were about process: … .] Everything
[NAME] said about the idea itself, however firmly, was an opinion at that
point in time. Lines marked *stated position* are the ones put most
firmly; they are not settled.

**A (interpretation)** are the agent's read of intent, mood, or values —
offered as context for the words, not as words the human said.
